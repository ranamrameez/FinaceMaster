Brokerage commission verified vs. real statement
These two commission tiers are the only figures on this page checked against a real broker statement, trade-by-trade. Everything in the next card is a separate, independently-editable line item — nothing is bundled together, so you can zero out or override any single charge without affecting the others.
Taxes & regulatory levies verify against your contract note
These are folded into every buy/sell cost, break-even and target-price calculation, as separate itemized line items — see the "Fee breakdown" on a position's detail card. Nothing here beyond the two commission tiers and SST has been checked against a real contract note; PSX explicitly says these levies vary by broker, so anything left at 0 simply isn't charged in this workbook until you fill it in. Same-day (intraday) round-trips aren't given special one-sided commission treatment here.
Capital Gains Tax NCCPL estimate
CGT is applied to the capital gain, never to the sale value — a loss owes Rs 0 CGT, it isn't made worse. CGT on PSX securities is computed and deducted separately by NCCPL, in aggregate across your whole account (not per-trade, not per-ticker the way commission is), so it's kept out of calcFee() and shown instead as a standalone "Est. CGT" figure on the Dashboard and on each position's detail card. This is a simplified estimate on realized gains only, using whichever holding-period/filer rate you set above — not NCCPL's actual per-lot computation (which applies its own standardized treatment of acquisition/disposal costs) — treat it as a ballpark, not a filing number.
