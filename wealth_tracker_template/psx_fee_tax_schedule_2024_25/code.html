Tax Impact Analysis

Elimination of Holding Incentives

Effective July 1, 2024, the tiered CGT structure based on holding periods has been abolished for new investments. All capital gains are now taxed at a flat rate of 15% for Filers and 45% for Non-Filers, regardless of whether the asset is held for 1 month or 5 years.
